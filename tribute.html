Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

About Him

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. He served as the 11th President of India and was known as the "Missile Man of India". His contributions to science, technology, and education inspired millions of people.

Achievements

  • 11th President of India (2002–2007)
  • Played a major role in India's missile development programs
  • Received the Bharat Ratna award
  • Author of the famous book "Wings of Fire"
  • Inspired students through speeches and education initiatives
